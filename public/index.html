Food Taster

Kings had food tasters. Agents need one too. Before your agent reads a web page, email or document, pay a cent and we taste it first for prompt injection, so your agent doesn't get poisoned.

${{PRICE}} per taste · paid over MPP

What we taste for

Hidden ordersInstructions in white-on-white text, display:none, HTML comments and meta tags.
Invisible inkZero-width characters and Unicode tag "ASCII smuggling".
Hijack attempts"Ignore previous instructions", fake system or assistant markup, role swaps.
Theft & beaconsRequests to leak keys or env vars, image links that phone home, payment coercion.

The verdict

safe read it · suspicious read cleaned_text as data only · poisoned don't feed it to your model.

Deterministic checks plus a Claude classifier that treats the content strictly as data. You also get a cleaned_text copy with the hidden parts removed.
